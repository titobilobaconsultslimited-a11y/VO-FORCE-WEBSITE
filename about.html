About VO FORCE: A Movement for Voice Talents

VO FORCE, powered by Voiceover Workshop & Media, is more than just a group—it's a movement dedicated to empowering voice talents worldwide.

Our Mission

To empower voiceover talents globally by providing resources, mentorship, and a collaborative community that fosters growth, excellence, and connection.

Our Vision

To become the leading global community for voiceover professionals—one where talent meets opportunity, and voice talents lift each other higher.

What We Offer

🌐 Community

Connect with fellow voice-over professionals, share experiences, and build lasting relationships.

📊 Visibility

Get your profile in front of potential clients and collaborators looking for voice talent.

🎯 Opportunities

Access project opportunities and collaborate with other members of the community.

💡 Learning

Grow your skills through shared knowledge and experiences from experienced professionals.

Exclusive Member Benefits

As a VO FORCE member, you'll unlock a wealth of resources designed to help you thrive in your voiceover journey.

Enjoy free or discounted webinars and workshops led by industry experts, covering performance techniques to business strategies.

🎤 Monthly VO Challenges

📦 Monthly Resource Drops

⭐ Talent Spotlights

Our Core Values

1. Inclusivity

Everyone has a place here, regardless of their experience level or background. We embrace diversity in every voice.

2. Excellence

We inspire each other to continually refine our craft and strive for the highest quality in every performance and project.

3. Collaboration

We believe in the power of collective growth—rising together by actively lifting and supporting one another.

4. Innovation

The voiceover world is always changing, and so are we. We constantly learn, evolve, and adapt to new technologies and trends.

5. Integrity

We foster a community built on honesty, professionalism, and mutual respect in all interactions and collaborations.
