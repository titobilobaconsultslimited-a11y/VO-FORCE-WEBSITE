Meet King E

Emmanuel Macaulay Afemikhe, widely known in the industry as King E, is a trailblazing Nigerian voiceover artist, renowned coach, and visionary entrepreneur. With over 13 years of dedicated experience, he has cemented his place as a foundational pillar of the African voice acting community.

As the Founder and CEO of Voiceover Workshop & Media, King E has established a leading training and production institute that equips emerging talents with the skills needed to thrive on the global stage. His passion for community building led him to create VOFORCE, a dynamic network empowering voiceover artists to collaborate, learn, and grow together.

King E is also the pioneering convener of The Voiceover Conference, recognised as the first and largest event of its kind in Africa. His commitment to talent development sparked the creation of the Nigerian voice acting contest, which has since evolved into the celebrated VO Star platform.

In September 2024, his massive contributions to the industry were officially honoured when he was awarded the prestigious Voiceover Legacy Award at the Africa Podcast and Voice Awards (APVA).

Whether he is behind the microphone delivering compelling audio or mentoring the next generation of African voices, King E's mission remains clear: to ensure that every great voice has the opportunity to be heard around the world.
